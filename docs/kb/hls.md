[Annex SL HLS] High-Level Structure for Integrated Management Systems
The High-Level Structure (HLS), defined in ISO's Annex SL, provides a common framework and identical core text for all ISO management system standards, meaning clauses 4 through 10 share the same numbering, titles, and structural requirements across ISO 9001, ISO 14001, and ISO 45001. This enables organizations to build a single integrated management system rather than maintaining separate parallel systems for quality, environment, and occupational health and safety. Cumplify leverages the HLS by implementing unified process maps where common requirements such as context analysis (4.1-4.2), leadership (5.1), roles and responsibilities (5.3), documented information (7.5), internal audit (9.2), and management review (9.3) are managed once and applied across all three standards. The ControlTower agent coordinates these shared clause processes, ensuring a single management review or internal audit program can satisfy all three standards simultaneously while flagging standard-specific requirements that need individual attention. Standard-specific requirements — such as ISO 45001's worker consultation and participation (5.4), hazard identification (6.1.2.1), and hierarchy of controls (8.1.2) — are handled by the respective ISO 45001 Domain Guru agent and OH&S-specific modules (M5, M11, M13), while quality-specific and environmental-specific clauses are managed by their own guru agents and domain modules.
