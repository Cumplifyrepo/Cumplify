[ISO 45001 4.1] Understanding the organization and its context
The organization must determine external and internal issues relevant to its OH&S purpose and strategic direction that affect its ability to achieve intended outcomes. This includes understanding workforce characteristics, work activities, geographic and regulatory environments, incident history, and the nature of hazards present. Cumplify's M6 module paired with the ContextCartographer agent captures and maintains these contextual factors, linking them to risk assessments and planning processes. Organizations should consider factors such as worker demographics, shift patterns, seasonal variations, subcontractor activities, and community proximity to operations. Regular review of context ensures the OH&S management system remains aligned with evolving organizational realities.

[ISO 45001 4.2] Understanding the needs and expectations of workers and other interested parties
The organization must identify interested parties beyond just customers, focusing on workers, regulators, contractors, unions, insurers, and affected communities whose needs and expectations are relevant to the OH&S management system. Workers hold a unique position as both interested parties and active participants in the system. Cumplify's M6 module maintains a stakeholder register that captures requirements from each party, tracks changes in their expectations, and links these to compliance obligations and operational controls. Understanding worker expectations around safety culture, consultation mechanisms, and the right to refuse dangerous work is fundamental to system effectiveness.

[ISO 45001 4.3] Determining the scope of the OH&S management system
The organization defines boundaries and applicability of its OH&S management system considering the context issues, interested party requirements, and planned or performed work activities. The scope must include activities, products, and services within the organization's control or influence that can impact OH&S performance. Cumplify's M1 module with DocStudio agent manages the scope statement as a controlled document, ensuring it covers all workplaces, worker types, and activities under organizational control. The scope should not be used to exclude hazards or circumvent legal requirements, and must be available as documented information to workers and other interested parties.

[ISO 45001 4.4] OH&S management system
The organization establishes, implements, maintains, and continually improves its OH&S management system including needed processes and their interactions in accordance with standard requirements. This involves applying the process approach to manage OH&S activities systematically rather than in isolation. Cumplify's ControlTower agent orchestrates process interactions across the system, ensuring hazard identification feeds into risk assessment which drives operational controls and performance monitoring. The system must account for the hierarchy of controls, worker participation, and the organization's commitment to eliminate hazards and reduce OH&S risks through planned, integrated processes.

[ISO 45001 5.1] Leadership and commitment
Top management demonstrates leadership by taking overall accountability for the protection of workers from work-related injury and ill health, ensuring the OH&S policy and objectives are established and compatible with strategic direction, and fostering a positive safety culture. This goes beyond delegation — leaders must actively promote consultation and participation, allocate necessary resources, and protect workers from reprisals when reporting incidents or hazards. Cumplify's M6 module provides an Executive dashboard showing leading and lagging OH&S indicators, enabling top management to demonstrate visible engagement. The ControlTower agent tracks leadership review actions and ensures management commitments translate into system improvements.

[ISO 45001 5.2] OH&S policy
The OH&S policy must include commitments to provide safe and healthy working conditions for the prevention of work-related injury and ill health, to eliminate hazards and reduce OH&S risks, and to continual improvement of the OH&S management system. The policy must also commit to consultation and participation of workers and their representatives. Cumplify's M1 module with DocStudio agent manages the OH&S policy as a controlled document with version history, approval workflows, and distribution tracking. The policy must be communicated to all workers in accessible languages, available to interested parties, and periodically reviewed for continuing suitability.

[ISO 45001 5.3] Organizational roles, responsibilities and authorities
Top management assigns and communicates responsibilities and authorities for relevant OH&S roles while retaining overall accountability for the management system. Key roles include ensuring the system conforms to requirements, reporting on system performance, and ensuring worker consultation and participation. Cumplify's M6 module maps roles such as EHS Manager, Supervisor, and Safety Representative to platform identity groups, enforcing role-based access controls and segregation of duties. The platform ensures that OH&S responsibilities are documented, communicated to all levels, and that workers understand their individual obligations including the authority to stop work presenting imminent danger.

[ISO 45001 5.4] Consultation and participation of workers
The organization must establish processes for consultation and participation of workers at all applicable levels and functions, providing timely access to clear and understandable information about the OH&S management system. Consultation involves seeking worker views before making decisions, while participation enables workers to actively contribute to hazard identification, risk assessment, and determination of controls. Cumplify's WorkerVoice agent facilitates this by providing accessible channels for worker input, tracking consultation records, and ensuring feedback loops are closed. Non-managerial workers must participate in determining OH&S policy commitments, assigning roles, identifying hazards, and investigating incidents. Barriers to participation such as language, literacy, shift patterns, or fear of reprisal must be identified and removed.

[ISO 45001 6.1.1] Actions to address risks and opportunities - General
When planning for the OH&S management system, the organization considers context issues, interested party requirements, scope, and determines risks and opportunities that need to be addressed to give assurance the system can achieve its intended outcomes and to prevent or reduce undesired effects. This includes OH&S risks arising from hazards, compliance risks, and risks to the management system itself such as failure to achieve objectives. Cumplify's M5 module with the RiskSentinel agent consolidates risks from context analysis, hazard identification, and compliance evaluation into a unified risk register. Planning must determine how to address these risks within OH&S management system processes and evaluate the effectiveness of actions taken.

[ISO 45001 6.1.2.1] Hazard identification
The organization establishes an ongoing and proactive process for hazard identification that considers how work is organized, social factors, leadership and culture, routine and non-routine activities, past incidents, potential emergency situations, people including those with access to the workplace, and other relevant factors. Hazard identification must account for infrastructure, equipment, materials, physical conditions of the workplace, product design, human factors, and how work is actually performed versus how it is prescribed. Cumplify's HazardScout agent systematically identifies hazards using configurable taxonomies covering physical, chemical, biological, ergonomic, and psychosocial categories. The agent prompts users through structured assessments for new processes, changed conditions, and near-miss analysis, feeding results directly into the M5 risk register for assessment and control determination.

[ISO 45001 6.1.2.2] Assessment of OH&S risks and other risks to the OH&S management system
The organization establishes processes to assess OH&S risks from identified hazards while taking into account the effectiveness of existing controls, and also assesses other risks related to the establishment and operation of the OH&S management system. Risk assessment must use defined methodologies that are proactive rather than reactive, applied systematically, and documented. Cumplify's M5 module with RiskSentinel agent provides configurable risk assessment matrices incorporating likelihood and severity, with the ability to evaluate residual risk after considering existing controls. The assessment output drives prioritization of actions, allocation of resources, and determination of operational controls according to the hierarchy of controls principle.

[ISO 45001 6.1.2.3] Assessment of OH&S opportunities and other opportunities
The organization assesses OH&S opportunities to enhance performance, including adapting work organization and conditions to workers, eliminating hazards at source, and opportunities to improve the management system itself. This positive framing ensures organizations look beyond risk reduction to actively improving working conditions and system effectiveness. Cumplify's M5 module captures opportunities alongside risks, enabling organizations to track initiatives that go beyond minimum compliance. Opportunities might include workplace redesign, technology adoption for hazard elimination, enhanced worker engagement mechanisms, or integration synergies with quality and environmental management systems.

[ISO 45001 6.1.3] Determination of legal requirements and other requirements
The organization determines and has access to applicable legal requirements and other requirements related to its OH&S hazards and management system, determines how these apply, and takes them into account when establishing and maintaining the system. Legal requirements include legislation, regulations, codes of practice, collective agreements, and voluntary commitments the organization subscribes to. Cumplify's M8 module with LegalLedger agent maintains an OH&S legal register that maps obligations to specific hazards, workplaces, and activities, providing alerts when requirements change. The system ensures that legal requirements are considered in hazard identification, risk assessment, operational controls, and compliance evaluation processes.

[ISO 45001 6.1.4] Planning action
The organization plans actions to address OH&S risks and opportunities, legal and other requirements, and prepares for and responds to emergency situations, integrating these actions into OH&S management system processes. Planning considers the hierarchy of controls, the need for changes to the system, and how to evaluate effectiveness of the planned actions. Cumplify's M5 module tracks action plans with assigned owners, target dates, resource requirements, and links to the originating risk or opportunity. The platform ensures traceability from hazard identification through risk assessment to control implementation and effectiveness verification, maintaining the complete chain in the audit trail.

[ISO 45001 6.2] OH&S objectives and planning to achieve them
The organization establishes measurable OH&S objectives at relevant functions, levels, and processes needed to maintain and improve the OH&S management system and achieve continual improvement of OH&S performance. Objectives must be consistent with the OH&S policy, take into account legal requirements and risk assessments, and consider consultation with workers. Cumplify's M7 module with the ObjectiveTracker agent manages objective-setting workflows, automatically linking objectives to policy commitments and risk assessment outputs. Planning to achieve objectives must determine what will be done, resources required, responsibilities, completion timescales, how results will be evaluated including indicators, and how actions will be integrated into business processes.

[ISO 45001 7.1] Resources
The organization determines and provides resources needed for the establishment, implementation, maintenance, and continual improvement of the OH&S management system. Resources include human resources with appropriate competencies, financial resources, technological and infrastructure resources, and time allocations. Planning must account for the capabilities and constraints of existing resources, including worker capacity and workload considerations that could themselves create OH&S risks. Cumplify's platform enables resource planning visibility by linking resource allocations to objectives, action plans, and operational controls, ensuring management review considers resource adequacy.

[ISO 45001 7.2] Competence
The organization determines necessary competence of workers that affects or can affect OH&S performance, ensures workers are competent on the basis of appropriate education, training, or experience, and takes actions to acquire and maintain needed competence. For OH&S specifically, workers must be competent to identify hazards and assess risks associated with their work activities. Cumplify's M13 module with the CompetenceKeeper agent maintains competence matrices mapped to job roles and OH&S responsibilities, tracks training records, manages certification renewals, and identifies competence gaps. Evidence of competence must be retained as documented information and the effectiveness of training actions must be evaluated.

[ISO 45001 7.3] Awareness
Workers must be made aware of the OH&S policy and relevant objectives, their contribution to effectiveness of the OH&S management system, implications of not conforming to requirements, incidents and outcomes of investigations relevant to them, and hazards and risks and actions determined that are relevant to them. Critically, workers must be aware of their ability to remove themselves from work situations they consider present imminent and serious danger to their life or health, and the arrangements for protecting them from undue consequences of doing so. Cumplify's M13 module manages awareness campaigns, tracks acknowledgments, and ensures role-relevant information reaches workers through appropriate channels considering language, literacy, and accessibility needs.

[ISO 45001 7.4] Communication
The organization determines internal and external communications relevant to the OH&S management system including what, when, with whom, and how to communicate, taking into account diversity aspects such as language, culture, literacy, and disability. Internal communication must ensure relevant OH&S information reaches all levels and functions, particularly enabling workers to contribute to continual improvement. Cumplify's M6 module manages communication matrices and channels, supporting multi-language delivery for diverse workforces. External communications must address legal requirements and satisfy interested parties while remaining consistent with information generated within the OH&S management system.

[ISO 45001 7.5] Documented information
The organization maintains and retains documented information required by the standard and determined as necessary for OH&S management system effectiveness, controlling it to ensure availability, suitability, and adequate protection. This includes controlling distribution, access, retrieval, storage, preservation, and disposition of OH&S records. Cumplify's M1 module with DocStudio agent and M4 RecordsVault manage the complete lifecycle of OH&S documented information with version control, approval workflows, and access controls. The platform ensures documents of external origin needed for OH&S planning and operation are identified and controlled, and that records remain legible, identifiable, and traceable throughout their retention period.

[ISO 45001 8.1.1] General (Operational planning and control)
The organization plans, implements, controls, and maintains processes needed to meet OH&S management system requirements by establishing criteria for the processes, implementing control of processes in accordance with the criteria, and maintaining documented information to the extent necessary. Controls must be adapted to workers and account for the hierarchy of controls when determining effective measures. Cumplify's operational control framework links each significant risk to specific control measures with defined monitoring parameters, responsibilities, and escalation triggers. Where controls involve behavioral requirements, the platform supports communication and reinforcement through awareness mechanisms and competence verification.

[ISO 45001 8.1.2] Eliminating hazards and reducing OH&S risks
The organization establishes processes for the elimination of hazards and reduction of OH&S risks using the hierarchy of controls in priority order: eliminate the hazard entirely, substitute with less hazardous processes or materials, use engineering controls and reorganization of work, use administrative controls including training, and provide adequate personal protective equipment. Higher-order controls are more effective and must be exhausted before relying on lower-order measures. Cumplify's M5 module enforces hierarchy-of-controls thinking by requiring justification when lower-level controls are selected, tracking the rationale as documented information. The platform ensures that PPE is genuinely a last resort and that elimination and substitution opportunities are systematically evaluated for every identified hazard.

[ISO 45001 8.1.3] Management of change
The organization establishes processes for the implementation and control of planned temporary and permanent changes that impact OH&S performance, including new products, services, or processes, changes to work organization, working conditions, equipment, or workforce, changes to legal requirements, and changes in knowledge of hazards and risks. The organization must review consequences of unintended changes and take action to mitigate any adverse effects. Cumplify's platform manages OH&S change assessments through structured workflows that evaluate the hazard implications of proposed changes before implementation, with the RiskSentinel agent flagging potential new risks requiring control determination.

[ISO 45001 8.1.4] Procurement
The organization establishes controls to ensure that procurement of products and services, including contractors and outsourced processes, conforms to its OH&S management system requirements. This includes establishing criteria for evaluating and selecting contractors based on their ability to meet the organization's OH&S requirements. Cumplify's M6 module manages contractor OH&S qualification records while operational controls ensure procurement specifications include OH&S requirements. The platform supports coordination activities with contractors on the organization's premises, ensuring hazard identification covers contractor activities and that emergency arrangements account for all parties present.

[ISO 45001 8.2] Emergency preparedness and response
The organization establishes, implements, and maintains processes needed to prepare for and respond to potential emergency situations including provision of first aid, OH&S training for emergency response, planned drills and exercises, evaluation of performance, and communication to all workers about their duties. Processes must account for the needs of all relevant interested parties and be coordinated with emergency services and neighboring organizations where applicable. Cumplify's M11 module with the EmergencyPlanner agent manages emergency plans, schedules and tracks drill execution, captures lessons learned, and ensures competence of emergency response personnel. Post-drill evaluations and actual emergency responses trigger review and improvement of emergency arrangements.

[ISO 45001 9.1.1] Monitoring, measurement, analysis and evaluation
The organization determines what needs to be monitored and measured including the extent to which legal requirements are fulfilled, activities and operations related to identified hazards and risks, progress toward OH&S objectives, and effectiveness of operational and other controls. Methods must ensure valid results, and the organization must evaluate OH&S performance and determine system effectiveness. Cumplify provides configurable monitoring dashboards that track leading indicators such as training completion, inspection rates, and hazard reports alongside lagging indicators including incident rates, severity measures, and lost-time frequencies. Analysis must consider trends and the context behind the numbers to drive meaningful improvement.

[ISO 45001 9.1.2] Evaluation of compliance
The organization establishes processes for evaluating fulfillment of its legal requirements and other requirements, determining the frequency and methods for compliance evaluation, and taking action if needed to maintain knowledge and understanding of its compliance status. This is not a one-time activity but an ongoing process that must respond to changes in legal requirements, organizational activities, and incident findings. Cumplify's M8 module with LegalLedger agent schedules compliance evaluations against the legal register, tracks evaluation results, identifies gaps requiring corrective action, and maintains the overall compliance status visible to management. Noncompliance findings are routed to the M2 nonconformity process for investigation and correction.

[ISO 45001 9.2] Internal audit
The organization conducts internal audits at planned intervals to provide information on whether the OH&S management system conforms to requirements and is effectively implemented and maintained. The audit program must consider the importance of the processes concerned, changes affecting the organization, and results of previous audits. Cumplify's M3 module with the LeadAuditor agent manages the audit program, supports audit planning with risk-based scheduling, facilitates evidence collection and finding documentation, and tracks corrective action closure. ISO 45001 specifically requires consultation with relevant workers on audit planning, ensuring the program addresses worker concerns and that auditors maintain objectivity and impartiality.

[ISO 45001 9.3] Management review
Top management reviews the OH&S management system at planned intervals to ensure its continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness, with inputs including status of previous review actions, changes in context and interested party needs, OH&S performance trends, adequacy of resources, effectiveness of worker participation, and opportunities for improvement. Specific OH&S inputs include incident trends, consultation and participation results, risk and opportunity status, and compliance evaluation outcomes. Cumplify's ReviewOrchestrator agent compiles review packages automatically from system data, presents dashboards for management discussion, and captures decisions with assigned responsibilities and timeframes. Outputs must include decisions related to continual improvement opportunities, resource needs, and any changes to the OH&S management system.

[ISO 45001 10.1] General (Improvement)
The organization determines opportunities for improvement and implements necessary actions to achieve the intended outcomes of its OH&S management system, enhancing OH&S performance through proactive measures rather than solely reactive corrections. Improvement encompasses both incremental advances and breakthrough changes in how the organization protects worker health and safety. Cumplify's platform surfaces improvement opportunities from multiple sources including audit findings, compliance evaluations, performance trend analysis, incident investigations, and worker consultation outputs. The ControlTower agent correlates these inputs to identify systemic patterns that indicate opportunities for fundamental improvement rather than isolated corrections.

[ISO 45001 10.2] Incident, nonconformity and corrective action
When an incident or nonconformity occurs, the organization reacts in a timely manner to control and correct it, deals with consequences, evaluates the need for corrective action to eliminate root causes, reviews existing risk assessments, determines and implements needed actions, reviews effectiveness of corrective actions taken, and makes changes to the OH&S management system if necessary. ISO 45001 specifically requires that workers participate in incident investigation and that investigations are conducted without blame, seeking systemic causes rather than individual fault. Cumplify's M2 module with CAPAGuru and IncidentInvestigator agents guide structured investigation processes, facilitate root cause analysis using appropriate methodologies, and track corrective actions through to verified effectiveness. All incident records feed into the immutable audit trail with hash-chain integrity.

[ISO 45001 10.3] Continual improvement
The organization continually improves the suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the OH&S management system by enhancing OH&S performance, promoting a culture that supports the system, promoting worker participation in implementing actions for continual improvement, and communicating relevant results to workers. Continual improvement considers technology advances, good practices, worker suggestions, changes in workforce composition, and lessons from other organizations. Cumplify's platform enables data-driven improvement by trending performance metrics over time, comparing against objectives, and identifying areas where the gap between current and desired OH&S performance is greatest. The system ensures improvement actions are planned, resourced, implemented, and their effectiveness verified in a closed-loop process.
