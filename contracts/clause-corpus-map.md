> **Purpose:** Canonical clause tuples for the Automated Reasoning clause-canon
> policy. Each entry is `{standard, edition, clause_number, title}` — no ISO
> body text (copyright compliance). Derived from `docs/architecture/iso-requirements-map.md`.
>
> Any `clauseRef` emitted by an agent MUST match a tuple in this file.
> A non-existent clause number or mismatched title is formally rejected at runtime.
