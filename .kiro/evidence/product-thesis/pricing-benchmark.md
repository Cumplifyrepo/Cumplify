## Synthesis
1. **Packaging laws:** employee bands × framework count (never per-seat);
   added frameworks = discrete declining add-on (+$5–7.5K or % uplift);
   annual-only default (monthly penalized 15–25%); pricing overwhelmingly
   quote-gated → transparent public pricing is a differentiator; list
   carries ~30% negotiation headroom.
2. **Recommended corridor for SMB tri-standard agentic SaaS:**
   entry single-standard **$6K–10K/yr**; mid all-three **$18K–30K/yr**
   (Vanta/Drata medians $20–25K; Thoropass 2-fw $35–50K); enterprise
   multi-site **$40K–75K/yr** (AuditBoard $40–150K; Vanta 201–500 emp
   $50–110K; MasterControl $25–100K; displaced-spend top $60K).
3. Position above commodity eQMS (automation value), below enterprise QMS
   at entry; the $6–30K SMB corridor is the open lane.
